Import from Xero reports

Run these two reports in Xero for the same period (the last 12 months works best) and export each one to Excel. The files are read here in your browser and aren't uploaded anywhere.

  1. Profit and Loss In Xero: Accounting → Reports → Profit and Loss. Fills your overheads and gross profit % on the Profit target tab.
  2. Income by Contact In Xero: Accounting → Reports → Income by Contact. Fills your client list with each client's average monthly fee.
